# 3D asset credits

All models and materials come from the free BlenderKit catalog (https://www.blenderkit.com).
They were downloaded at 0.5K/1K texture resolution, decimated, and baked in Blender into one
mesh + one material per item (colour / roughness / metal / AO atlas). They are packed in
models/furniture.glb and models/trees.glb (Draco-compressed, WebP textures) and textures/*.jpg.

Licences: CC0 assets are public domain. BlenderKit Royalty Free assets may be used commercially
without attribution; reselling the assets themselves (as models or asset packs) is not allowed.

## Trees and shrubs (models/trees.glb)

A matching set of stylized low-poly trees. Their procedural leaf and bark shaders were baked into
small colour + AO textures per model and the facets keep flat normals. The two shrubs are built
from canopy blobs of the oaks, so they share the same facets and leaf shader. Each model is drawn
as an instanced mesh.

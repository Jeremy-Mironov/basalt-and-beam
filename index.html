01/ 07 · Service

Pre-Construction & Design

Every successful build is won before ground is broken. We coordinate architects and engineers, run feasibility and site surveys, secure permits and deliver a transparent, line-item budget before the first stake goes in.

  • Site evaluation, surveys & feasibility
  • Permitting, zoning & HOA approvals
  • Line-item estimates & value engineering
Learn more about pre-construction and design

02/ 07 · Service

Site Work & Foundation

A building is only as good as what it stands on. We handle excavation, grading and utilities, then form and pour engineered footings and slabs to spec, inspected and documented before anything goes on top.

  • Excavation, grading & drainage
  • Engineered footings & slab-on-grade
  • Utility trenching & under-slab rough-ins
Learn more about site work and foundations

03/ 07 · Service

Structural Framing

Framing is where plans become rooms. Our crews frame plumb, square and true, with engineered lumber, continuous load paths and sheathing that is nailed off and inspected before a single wall is covered.

  • Wood & light-gauge steel framing
  • Load-bearing walls, beams & headers
  • Sheathing, weather barrier & inspections
Learn more about structural framing

04/ 07 · Service

Interior Build-Out

With the shell closed in, we run plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation and drywall on one coordinated schedule, so rooms take shape quickly and cleanly without the usual handoff delays. The same team handles commercial tenant improvements.

  • MEP rough-in, insulation & drywall
  • Trim carpentry, doors & built-ins
  • Flooring, paint & lighting
Learn more about interior build-out

05/ 07 · Service

Windows, Doors & Exteriors

The envelope protects everything inside it. We install energy-efficient windows and doors, flash every opening correctly and finish exteriors in siding, stone and trim that look sharp on day one and hold up for decades.

  • ENERGY STAR® windows & entry doors
  • Lap siding, fiber-cement & stone
  • Covered porches, decks & exterior trim
Learn more about windows, doors and exteriors

06/ 07 · Service

Kitchen & Bath Remodeling

Kitchens and baths are where craftsmanship shows most. We design and install custom cabinetry, stone counters, tile and fixtures with exacting care, whether it is part of a new build or a full remodel of the rooms you use every day.

  • Custom cabinetry & kitchen islands
  • Quartz, stone & tile surfaces
  • Fixtures, freestanding tubs & walk-in showers
Learn more about kitchen and bath remodeling

07/ 07 · Service

Roofing & Final Finishes

We close out with a roof built for your climate and a punch list we actually finish. Landscaping, final inspections and a detailed walkthrough mean you get the keys to a home that is complete, not almost done.

  • Asphalt, metal & standing-seam roofing
  • Landscaping, hardscape & exterior lighting
  • Final inspections, walkthrough & warranty
Learn more about roofing and final finishes

Process

Four steps. One point of contact.

You get a dedicated project manager, a fixed schedule and weekly updates from the first meeting to the final walkthrough.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A free on-site or virtual meeting to understand your goals, scope, budget and timeline, with honest advice on what it will take.

  2. 02

    Design & Estimate

    Plans, selections and a fixed, line-item estimate you can compare. No allowances buried in the fine print.

  3. 03

    Build

    Permitted, inspected work with a clean, safe jobsite, a shared schedule and photo updates every week.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough & Warranty

    A detailed punch list, a final walkthrough and a written workmanship warranty that we stand behind.
